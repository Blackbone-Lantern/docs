# Engineering Philosophy

Blackbone Software builds products with a long-term mindset.

Our goal is not simply to create software that works today, but to build software that remains understandable, maintainable and enjoyable to evolve for years to come.

Engineering is about making intentional decisions—not collecting technologies.

---

## Our Principles

### Build intentionally

Every significant technical decision should have a clear purpose.

We choose technologies because they solve real problems—not because they are fashionable.

---

### Understand before optimizing

Before changing, optimizing or replacing an existing solution, we first seek to understand it.

Understanding leads to better engineering decisions than assumptions.

---

### Prefer clarity over cleverness

Readable, well-structured and understandable code is more valuable than clever code.

We optimize for maintainability rather than short-term brilliance.

---

### Document important decisions

Architectural decisions should remain understandable months and years later.

Significant engineering decisions are documented using Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).

---

### Every rule should solve a real problem

Processes, conventions and guidelines exist to improve engineering—not to create bureaucracy.

If a rule no longer solves a meaningful problem, it should be questioned and, if necessary, improved.

---

### Think in products, not projects

Projects have an end.

Products evolve.

We design software that can continuously grow together with its users.

---

### Quality is built in

Quality is never something added at the end.

Testing, documentation, code reviews and automation are integral parts of the development process from the very beginning.

---

### Learn continuously

Technology evolves.

Good engineers evolve with it.

Every project is an opportunity to improve our engineering skills, challenge assumptions and discover better solutions.

---

## Our Goal

We strive to build software that is:

- understandable
- maintainable
- testable
- scalable
- accessible
- secure
- enjoyable to work on

Our success is measured not only by delivering features, but by building products that remain reliable, adaptable and valuable over time.

---

## Living Document

This philosophy is intentionally concise.

It should evolve together with our products, our experience and our understanding of great software engineering.

The goal is not to create more rules.

The goal is to make better decisions.
